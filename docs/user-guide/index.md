# TermConnect User Guide

For WordPress site owners and content editors who configure and use the plugin. To install it and enable your first taxonomy, start with [Getting started](../#getting-started). For the PHP API, hooks, REST fields, and settings keys, see the [Developer Reference](../developer-reference/).

- **[Concepts](concepts.html)** — when peer networks make sense, term equivalence and external identifiers, and Max FOAF Depth.
- **[Peer Sites & Feeds](peer-sites-feeds.html)** — namespaces and link types, WordPress peer sites, and RSS/Atom feeds.
- **[Displaying Peer Posts](displaying-peer-posts.html)** — `[tc_peer_posts]`, the interactive `[tc_peer_browse]` topic browser, their blocks, and blending local and peer posts with `scope="all"`.
- **[Archives & Suggestions](archives-suggestions.html)** — the "See also" section on term archives and the link-suggestion form.
- **[Delegated Identity](delegated-identity.html)** — deferring a term's authoritative definition to a matching term on a peer site.
- **[Shared Vocabularies](shared-vocabularies.html)** — mirroring a peer's term structure into your local taxonomy.
- **[Working with HyperTaxonomy](working-with-hypertaxonomy.html)** — what becomes available when HyperTaxonomy is active too.
- **[Settings, Cache & Export](settings-export.html)** — global settings, cache tools, and export/import.
